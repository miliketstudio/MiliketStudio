Turn a YouTube video into slides

Paste a link, run the notebook on Google Colab, and get the video plus a PDF and a PowerPoint of every slide. Free, with no card or payment.

The download runs on Google’s computers, not in this page, because YouTube blocks website servers.

How to run it

  1. Open the notebook The button above opens it in Colab. Sign in with any Google account.
  2. Run cell 1 It installs the tools. This takes about a minute and is needed once per session.
  3. Paste your link into cell 2 and run it Works for one video or a whole playlist. Change the settings below first if you want.
  4. Wait for it to finish A zip downloads, or the files appear in your Google Drive when Save to Drive is on.
  5. Run cell 3 to flip through the slides Pick a video, drag the slider, or press play.

What you get

  • One folder per video
    • slides.pdfevery slide as a page
    • slides.pptxPowerPoint you can edit
    • frames.zipthe slide images
    • video.mp4the full video, unless you chose slides only

Settings in cell 2

MODE
"both" saves slides and video. "slides" skips the video file. "video" downloads the video only.
SENSITIVITY
A lower number gives more slides, a higher number gives fewer. The default is 3.0.
SAVE_TO_DRIVE
Set to True to save into a video_archive folder in your Drive instead of downloading a zip. Use it for big playlists, since a zip of 28 videos often fails in the browser. Drive’s free 15 GB applies.

Good to know

  • Files vanish when the Colab session resets. Download them or save to Drive right after a run.
  • Every video fails with “Sign in to confirm you’re not a bot”? YouTube is blocking that session. In Colab choose Runtime, then Disconnect and delete runtime, and run again.
  • Colab asks you to authorize GitHub? That happens when the yt-archive repository is private. Approve it once, or make the repository public.
